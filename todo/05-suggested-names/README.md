# Suggested Game Names

**21 October 2026. Naming preparation only.** These are suggestions for team review. The final game title remains undecided; H.O.P.E. Colony remains the settlement name.

| Suggested title | Meaning and fit |
|---|---|
| **SCRAPBOUND: Last Hope** | Recommended option. Connects the creatures bound in scrap armor, humanity's dependence on salvaged resources, and H.O.P.E. Colony |
| **SCRAPBOUND** | Short form of the recommended option; suitable if the team prefers a single-word title |
| **Wreckage Rising** | Emphasizes discarded material becoming the shell of an emerging biological threat |
| **Orbit of Consequence** | Connects the orbital setting with the consequences of neglect and delayed responsibility |
| **H.O.P.E.: Reclaim Earth** | Places the colony and the campaign's recovery mission at the center |
| **Scavengers of Tomorrow** | Connects the organisms, salvage pilots and humanity's uncertain future |
| **The World We Wasted** | A darker story-focused title about environmental damage and accountability |

## Recommended title and tagline pairing

**SCRAPBOUND**

> We kept throwing it away. Until it came back for us.

Optional subtitle: **Last Hope**.

## Review notes

- Select the final wording, capitalization and subtitle before producing a logo or title card.
- Name availability has not been researched; these suggestions are not a uniqueness or trademark-clearance claim.
- Record the team's selected title in the project overview and progress tracker when a choice is made.
- This folder does not authorize changing the executable name, menus, code, assets or build configuration.
