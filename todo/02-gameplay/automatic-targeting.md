# Automatic targeting and Blink safety - 25 October 2026

Q33 confirms that automatic attack abilities choose enemies themselves. Primary weapons retain mouse aim; learning an ability never rotates the player toward its target, moves the mouse, fires the primary weapon or introduces a manual skill button. This is a written specification, not runtime conversion of the existing 18-ability prototype. Target roster has 19 abilities including Shield Detonation; [game design](../../context/game-design.md#25-october-automatic-targeting-and-blink-defaults) owns numbers. Source inspection also finds current Collapse emits full victim HP, including bosses; finite boss damage below is required future work, not an already-fixed behavior.

## Common query, selection and lifecycle rules

Only running/alive combat activates abilities. Pause, cards, console capture, hub, checkpoint choice and terminal presentation freeze their simulation. Persistent shields/blades/droids retain state during suspension; a new run resets ranks, targets, casts, slots and clocks. Ordinary return-protection gating still applies; do not use automatic casts to bypass the selected protected-return policy.

Valid enemy: alive, hostile, currently damageable by that ability, center inside the current visible combat viewport, inside the named range and in the same legal combat region. Exclude friendly drones/clone, decorative traffic, scenery, pickups, dead/despawning entities and immune targets that the effect cannot benefit from hitting. Bosses are ordinary valid finite-damage targets, not instant-erasure candidates. Range checks use center-to-center squared distance, inclusive at the boundary. Actual hit geometry additionally includes the appropriate victim collision shape; acquisition range is not secretly increased by victim size.

Use existing spatial queries and stable entity IDs. Aiming/projectile/beam acquisition and each chain segment require unobstructed authored solid geometry; do not shoot through a wall because targeting can see an entity ID. Ground-zone centers must be legal, visible and within placement range. Area victims use the real effect geometry and obstacle policy, not a hard count limit. Already committed effects remain at their captured world position even if the camera later moves; off-screen target acquisition is still forbidden.

`Pick` is the single-target selector. Q34 confirms nearest valid enemy, with no boss-priority override. Use lexicographically smallest stable entity ID on equal distance; never unordered-container order. Distance is measured from the attack origin named in the matrix (normally the player, or the clone for its own attacks); "threat" does not add a hidden danger score. Droids retain separately confirmed spread fire. Group attacks keep density selection. A boss wins a single-target selection only when it is the nearest valid target or wins the ordinary equal-distance tie.

Group selector: examine the nearest 32 legal enemy-center anchors, ordered by distance then ID; for each, count legal affected enemies inside the captured effect shape using spatial queries. Choose greatest count, then nearest anchor, then ID. The anchor-search bound limits placement evaluation only, never damage victims, rank, blade count or droid army size. If there is one valid enemy, it is a valid group. A legal enemy center is the placement fallback; if none exists, remain ready.

An inactive ready ability validates its trigger and legal target/placement before `begin`. No target means no cooldown spend, cast visuals, sound or gameplay RNG draw; retry after 0.1 combat seconds or earlier on a relevant entity/pickup/threat change. First acquisition becomes ready on the next eligible running update; owned-rank purchases/picks preserve debt and live casts. Commit one cast, snapshot rank/damage/radius/duration/interval and start its normal cooldown at activation, matching the existing begin-time debt model. No overlapping second instance of the same timed ability; restart only after the active window ends and cooldown is ready.

Single-target live attacks may refresh their lock every 0.1 combat seconds and immediately when it becomes invalid, without changing firing cadence. No target during an already committed window emits no attack; its existing duration/cooldown continues, with no refund, banked missed shots or retroactive burst. Delayed blasts and stationary zones retain their committed center. Target handles are IDs revalidated at use, not pointers kept across removals. Offensive rate/rank correctness and extreme-load scheduling remain PREP-14/T04 integration work, not certified by this matrix.

## Droids: spread fire and destroyed-slot recovery

Use persistent stable slot IDs; no army-count cap or limited active inventory. Rebuild target reservations every 0.2 combat seconds and on target invalidation, in stable slot order. For each droid, order legal targets by current assigned-droid count, distance from that droid, then entity ID; increment the chosen target's reservation. With fewer enemies than droids, multiple droids may share a target. A lock is stable between reservation updates unless invalid; no cursor following or rapid order-dependent target flips.

Droids may follow an eligible visible enemy within P of the player, using existing speed/standoff and legal movement. Fire only within 35 units of that droid with a clear path. Otherwise approach legally or follow the ship without firing. No target teleports a droid or causes a map-wide shot. Allied droids are nonblocking to each other; legal following does not require adding an army cap.

Acquiring a rank adds its earned slot on the next eligible field update, not in the hub/card panel. Living units keep HP and fire debt. Destroyed slots start independent 20-combat-second timers; a slot produces one replacement when due, never the entire army. Pause/cards/hub/protection ownership must preserve timers according to the run lifecycle; entering a shop, buying a rank or refreshing target reservations is not a free repair/rebuild. New/rebuilt units snapshot current rank tuning; existing units do not heal when the rank changes. Reset clears all slots/reservations/timers.

## Blink: exact safe-candidate contract

Adopt existing proposed beta tuning under delegation: cooldown `max(12, 30 * 0.95^(rank-1))` combat seconds; travel `35 * (1 + 0.15 * min(rank-1, 4))` world units; fixed 2 s direct-hit protection, separate from Phase Shift and ordinary return protection. Current prototype 4 s/manual Blink is not this target.

Only a validated positive direct projectile/contact damage event can ask for Blink. Existing debug invulnerability, prior damage guards and active Blink/return protection reject hits first, without spending Blink. Existing non-spatial status ticks, boundaries and prior-frame damage do not trigger it. A broken shield cannot be repaired by it; successful interception precedes shield/hull debit.

Candidates: 3 radii (one-third, two-thirds, full travel), each at world-space headings 0/45/90/135/180/225/270/315 degrees, starting along +X; fixed ring-then-heading order, 24 total and no RNG. Require finite coordinates, whole player collider inside visible viewport and legal arena, and no solid intersecting either destination or the straight legality segment from current position. This conservative segment check prevents crossing a closed wall/barricade; it is not a collision sweep applying hits/pickups along a teleport path.

Reject candidate if its collider expanded by a 2-unit safety margin overlaps a damaging body or known active/imminent hazard. For hostile projectiles and moving bodies, test their swept predicted path over the next 0.25 combat seconds against the expanded player collider. Include authored attack geometry whose active interval begins inside that horizon; unknown future enemy decisions are not a guaranteed prediction. A candidate needs no camera jump or arena escape.

Among passing candidates choose greatest minimum predicted clearance, then greatest separation from the triggering threat's path, then lowest fixed candidate index. No candidate passing means no movement, protection or cooldown; process the original hit normally. Success consumes the triggering projectile/contact event once, moves once, snapshots cooldown and gives the 2 s protected window. Other direct hits in that window are rejected without chain-Blinking. Do not sweep teleport travel for free loot, enemy contact or kills; ordinary destination pickup checks may happen later.

Rank changes preserve existing debt/protection duration, using new cooldown/travel only at the next successful trigger. Statuses remain; no hull/shield/energy refill, offensive impact or camera/primary-aim snap. New Run resets readiness and all transient guards. This safety specification does not promise every dangerous situation has a legal escape.
