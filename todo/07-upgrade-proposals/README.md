## Confirmed direction

- Start with the normal simple-bullet cannon. Cannon model/tier upgrades are purchased only at the mothership.
- Follow one weapon branch per run. A purchased branch locks out its siblings for that run.
- Build toward five weapon tiers with machine-gun, sniper and shotgun identities.
- Purchased attribute improvements grant **25% of the corresponding field card's gain**. Shield stays obtainable/upgradable through field cards as well as the mothership.
- All skills activate automatically. The 23 October field return portals replace planned manual Return Home: F inside a portal is an interaction; normal movement, aim and primary-fire controls remain separate.
- Blink is **Legendary**, reacts to an incoming hit, prevents that triggering damage and moves the player to a safer position within the visible screen. Its cooldown should be around 30 seconds and improve with rank.
- Boss designs, mode schedules, final names, hub/arena layouts and broader economy review are **deferred by the owner**. Preserve their tasks without requesting those decisions now.
