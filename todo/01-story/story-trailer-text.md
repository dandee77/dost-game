# Story Trailer — Text Only

We kept throwing it away.

Until it came back for us.

For generations, humanity filled the Earth with waste and left its broken machines drifting among the stars. As the planet warmed, we searched for something that could clean up after us.

So we created the Scavengers.

Living organisms that consumed our garbage and recovered what could still be used. They worked on Earth and in the stations above it.

They bought us time.

We spent it making more waste.

In 2718, scientists announced another breakthrough: an atmospheric treatment that could help our wounded world recover.

Five years later, we released it.

We never understood what it would do to the life we had already created.

The Scavengers changed.

Their hunger became relentless. They stopped sorting and began taking everything within reach. What they could digest fed them. What remained, they bound around their bodies.

Plates became armor. Cables held their shells together.

With each generation, the creatures that survived became better adapted to a world buried in our waste.

Then they came for the people still living in it.

Within nine years, only one in a hundred remained.

The survivors fled to stations they could still defend. They patched the walls, restored the lights, and gave their refuge a name.

H.O.P.E.

Now, you are one of its cadets.

Recover the materials that keep your people alive. Fight through the wreckage. Reach the contaminated nests where the strongest creatures gather—and open a path for those who will begin the cleanup.

Earth is still below us.

There is still something worth saving.

We survived the consequences.

Now we must change.
