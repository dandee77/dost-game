All main pieces connected through plausible struts and hinges. Large concave notches and generous negative space between projections. Clearly longer in one axis; avoid round, ball-like, oval or roughly circular piles. Recognizably dead orbital machinery, not a functional symmetrical spacecraft, not rock, not a garbage-can pile. Exactly one intact static sprite and one design, no extra models, no animation or sheet. Genuine transparent background. Logical canvas 96x96 pixels, visible wreck fits approximately 48x40 pixels centered at (48,48); large clear transparent margins. Same coarse pixel density as reference: one-pixel solid dark contours, flat clusters of 2-3 tones, each small feature economical and readable at native scale. Strict limited palette: #000000 #2E222F #3E3546 #625565 #966C6C. Distinguish the panel's dark square cells with lighter straight metal rails. Upper-left highlights, deeper lower-right shadows. If image output is enlarged, show an exact nearest-neighbor enlargement of the logical pixel canvas with uniformly square pixels. No antialiasing, gradients, blurred edges, 3D render, realistic texture, glow, stars, ground shadow, labels, lettering, branding, watermark or background checkerboard. Prioritize the unmistakably crooked, angular satellite-scrap silhouette.
